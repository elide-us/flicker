## Scope

The fourteen schools of magic have stood for roughly one hundred thousand years (Book VI). Book IV's word-grammar (noun + verb core, adjective/adverb modifiers, shape) is the *language* magic is expressed in — it is not an invitation for a player to invent an arbitrary new combination from nothing. Each school holds an established canon of **actual spells that already exist within the fiction**, most of them older than any name now attached to them. This book is that canon: the Grimoire. "Researching" a spell (Book IV) means excavating and unlocking one of these existing entries, not authoring a new one.

This book does not replace Book IV — the grammar, the noun/verb/adjective/adverb/shape dictionaries, and the ring-mastery structure stay there. This book is the *content* the grammar expresses: named, ring-gated, school-specific spells.

## Entry Format

**Ruled target: bestiary grade** (2026-07-05, ledger 19843543). A finished Grimoire entry reads like a Book VII creature entry — multiple sections and tables, real lore weight — not the compressed sketch the Fire sample below uses. The Grimoire is **lore-generation space**: every spell is an artifact of a hundred-thousand-year tradition, and its entry should carry that. Working section list for the full format (to be finalized when the book unblocks):

- **Name** — the spell's Words-of-Power string and its plain-language gloss (the grammar lives here; no separate breakdown).
- **School** — one line: color, Domain world, and discipline (e.g. Fire (Red), Sanguia — Pyromancy).
- **Ring** — the mastery rank that unlocks it (Book IV's ring structure).
- **Delivery / Cast time / Fizzle** — delivery per Book I's attack taxonomy (point-blank (touch), line AE, …); cast time and fizzle chance are both load-bearing rows (values blocked with the rest of the combat economy).
- **Effect** — what it does, mechanically, with stat/tier tables where the combat integration provides them.
- **Casting Cost & Reagents** — the cost table AND its rationale: *why* this spell costs what it costs, why these reagents — the economics as lore (blocked on the combat-tier integration; ledger 1E5C0E3C).
- **History** — where the spell came from: the tradition, era, or figure that first worked it, when the record survives (rare — see Book VI, "ancient immortals... names lost to the centuries"); when it doesn't, the silence is itself the correct answer, told as such.
- **Anti-color notes** — where this spell sits against its school's weakness colors (see Anti-Colors, below): what it does that a weak school envies, or how it strains to imitate a stronger color's ease.
- **Unlock, beyond location [STUB]** — per Elideus, unlocking a spell may carry a gameplay requirement beyond simply reaching the school that holds it. What that requirement is remains undesigned; see Open Questions.

## Anti-Colors — the Shape of Weakness [PROPOSED, 2026-07-05]

**Every school is nearly self-sufficient — so the design lives in the weaknesses** (ledger 19843543). With hundreds of spells and much overlap among schools, coverage is not what distinguishes a school; its **weakness colors** are.

**The opposite is geometric** (ruled 2026-07-05, ledger 5E86ED4A). The ring structure itself defines it: the **4th ring opens a school's direct neighbors** on the septisigil (Black skipped), and the **7th ring opens the next tier out** — four colors reached through synergy, and **always exactly one color left unreached: the school's opposite.** The leftovers pair mutually:

| Opposite pair | The opposition |
|---|---|
| **White ↔ Orange** | Light/Prophecy vs Chaos/Transformation — order against the transgression that broke it |
| **Yellow ↔ Blue** | Air/Lightning vs Water/Illusion — the storm against the rain it hides |
| **Red ↔ Green** | Fire/Blood vs Earth/Life — the flame against the vine; the mountain and the plow |

Black, as always, stands outside the system. And the alignment with the canon is **by construction, not coincidence** (Elideus, 2026-07-05): these are the creation myth's balance pairs verbatim — Book VI's First Age telling finds Red *"seeking balance to Green"* ("the soil needed the blood of life"), finds Blue *"balancing Yellow"* (rain hidden in the storm clouds), and White↔Orange is the First Titan's transgression itself — because **the septisigil and the myth are one authored design**. Elideus hand-drew `septisigil.svg` to work the entire system out, and developed the creation myth in line with that geometry. The figure on the page, the sky's hidden Chalice, the synergy rings, and the myth's balances are the same deliberate structure — all reflections of the same Prism, on purpose.

**Weakness is a gradient with workarounds, never a ban.** A school can usually approximate what it is weak at — slowly, expensively, or through cleverness — and those straining entries are some of the most expressive content in the book. The register example: **Water cannot burn.** It warms slowly, hauling heat in on ocean currents; it cannot make flame at all — *unless it bends light through a water-lens illusion* and focuses the sun into fire that was never Water's to make. Each school reaching past its edge by paying more, waiting longer, or being cleverer than the color that owns the effect — that is what anti-color entries are for. *(Note: under the geometry, Blue's true opposite is Yellow — Red is Blue's 7th-ring far-synergy partner, per Sanguine Ember — so where exactly the mechanical counter attaches is the open question below.)*

**[OPEN QUESTION]** Which relation carries the mechanical counter — the geometric opposite alone, the excavated two-per-color web (`sources/combat-party-scale-and-damage-math-chatgpt.md`; the geometric opposite is always one of its two), or a gradient across opposite and far colors — is Elideus's explicit "not sure yet," reserved for the combined combat session (ledger 1E5C0E3C). The septisigil now carries three relation layers on one figure: perimeter adjacency = synergy, inner weave = genealogy, leftover = opposition.

## Sample School: Fire (Red)

Chosen as the calibration sample because Book IV's dictionaries are furthest along for it. Fire is one of Red's two Domains/schools (the other is Blood); Red's shard world is Sanguia — the Mountain, whose lava is the blood of fire, drawn in the sky as the Anvil (Book VI). *(Entries 1–4 below carry the ruled format, calibrated 2026-07-05 — Kindle is Elideus's own reference edit; entries 5–6 remain in the old compressed sketch pending the same treatment. The rings 1–3 progression is Elideus's ruled teaching arc: learn to make a fire, then to send a lesser one, then to feed a greater. All cost tables are absent by rule — blocked on the combat-tier integration, ledger 1E5C0E3C.)*

### 1. Kindle
*In Flam — "Create Fire"*

| | |
|---|---|
| **School** | Pyromancy - Fire (Red) |
| **Ring** | 1st |
| **Delivery** | Point-blank (touch) |
| **Cast time** | Instant |
| **Fizzle** | Critical Fail Only |

**Effect.** A cupped ember in the caster's palm — enough to light a torch, a fuse, or a hearth. Candle-grade light and warmth; dies if the caster takes a solid hit. Not a weapon. *[Values blocked pending combat integration.]*

**Casting Cost & Reagents.** *[BLOCKED.]* The cheapest working in the school — at or below the lowest consumable grade. The tradition's rationale: fire wants to exist; the pinch of tinder is courtesy, not fuel.

**History.** The first working every apprentice is given, and older than every tradition that gives it. No school claims it — the ones who first taught it are the long-lived whose names the centuries have eaten (Book VI).

**Anti-color notes.** The Green seam shows at the first ring: the ember that warms the hand kills the seedling it touches. Fire never mends or grows — that art belongs to the vine (Biomancy, Book V).

**Unlock.** *[STUB]* Taught on an apprentice's first day; should stay free of any extra requirement. Known to all characters that start with Pyromancy skill.

*Bet Flam — "Lesser Fire"*

|---|---|
| **School** | Pyromancy - Fire (Red) |

| **Delivery** | Targeted, short range |
| **Cast time** | Very short, interruptible |

**Anti-color notes.** At range the Green seam is unchanged: fire sent is still fire — it takes; it does not tend.

### 4. Wall of Flame
*Vas Flam Rectus — "Greater Fire, in a Straight Line"*

| | |
|---|---|
| **School** | Pyromancy - Fire (Red) |
| **Ring** | 4th |
| **Delivery** | Line area-effect, placed; persists, burning on ticks |
| **Cast time** | Full cast, interruptible |
| **Fizzle** | Standard |

**Effect.** A standing wall of fire along a chosen line, burning whatever crosses it for its duration. An honest hazard in the fairness-law sense — danger as a legible place, entered by choice (Book I). *[Duration, tick damage, and dimensions blocked.]*

**Casting Cost & Reagents.** *[BLOCKED.]* The school's first expensive working — a persistent zone, not a moment of flame — carrying a real consumable requirement that a higher ring can cheapen (Book IV, Reagent Grades). A wall must be fed for as long as it stands.

**History.** Military in every surviving account, domestic in none. This book's own worked example (Book IV, Core Mechanics), and the working soldiers of the Red realms swear by the Anvil overhead. The drill-yard saying: *a wall is a promise; do not raise one you cannot keep fed.*

**Anti-color notes.** The opposed doctrines meet openly here: Fire's wall denies ground; Green's hedge takes it.

## Open Questions

- **The build-out is blocked** (ruled 2026-07-05, ledger 19843543): the other thirteen schools are not to be written until the combat-tier mechanics integrate — casting costs, reagent economics, and the anti-color balance land together in the combined combat/items/stats session (ledger 1E5C0E3C), and entries without them are not source material. The Fire sample calibrates grammar and voice only, and is itself below the ruled bestiary-grade format.
- **Scale is ruled in direction** (ledger 19843543): **hundreds of spells**, with much overlap among schools — every school nearly self-sufficient. Per-school roster size and the overlap conventions (does each school carry its own themed version of shared effects, per the old "every effect in ≥3 schools" principle?) are unspecified.
- **The anti-color structure** — which colors are each school's weakness colors (the excavated counter web is the candidate), and how weakness expresses mechanically (cost multipliers, ring penalties, ban-with-workaround) — is the counter-web session's subject.
- **Shapes — RESOLVED in structure, words pending** (Elideus, 2026-07-05). Shapes stay spoken words, but **there is no ring unlock for shapes** — that gating "was always going to be problematic in gameplay," and with the Grimoire established there is no need for a gated shape grammar: a spell is excavated whole, its shape baked in (Book IV, Shapes, updated). The shape **vocabulary expands broadly** — abstract geometry (spiral, crescent, chevron) *and* concrete forms (wall, arrow, ballistic, slice, and on), each coined Latin-esque when first needed. Still pending: the actual new words — including the wall-word for entry 4 (*Vas Flam Rectus* stands in until it is coined; candidate *Murus*, unruled — and per Elideus, definitively not *Fencius*).
- **Unlock requirements beyond physical presence** — Elideus: interesting gameplay requirements beyond simply reaching the school that holds a spell are wanted, but undesigned. Candidate directions (not proposed, just named as a space): a quest, a reagent sacrifice, a demonstrated prerequisite spell, a celestial timing window (consistent with celestial primacy, ledger 3312B2AB). None of these are ruled.
- **Ring count beyond the 7th** stays open per Book IV (ledger B92EE74A); this book cannot resolve it either. Note that rings beyond the 7th are now framed as **expansion / "forbidden fruit" territory** (Book IV) — the domain of world-shifting, Long-Term-Objective magic (Book I, LTO layer), not ordinary Grimoire entries. The sample above tops out at the 7th ring for exactly that reason; anything past it is a different kind of content.
- **Ordinary travel between the seven worlds is solved (canon, 2026-07-05).** A character reaches Sanguia (or any Domain world) to study its Grimoire by **trivial travel magic** — the present civilization figured it out long ago — never routed through the guarded, quest-gated Advent gates (Book V, cosmology section). Its concrete form is still [STUB], but its triviality is ruled, so this book can assume any school is reachable.
